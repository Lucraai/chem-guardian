# GB 30871-2022 Special Work Safety Example

## Hot Work

Hot work usually requires confirmation of the work scope, approval status, on-site monitoring, fire protection measures, and gas detection before execution.

The work permit should define the operation area, validity period, approval flow, monitoring responsibilities, and emergency response arrangements.

The site owner should verify that fire extinguishing equipment, isolation measures, and monitoring personnel are in place before work begins.

## Confined Space

Confined space work usually requires ventilation, detection, guardian assignment, emergency rescue readiness, and work approval, with continuous risk monitoring.

## Temporary Power Use

Temporary power use usually requires inspection of wiring, protection devices, approval scope, validity period, and site patrol requirements to avoid extra risks.
